# Chapter 13: Limitations and Future Scope

## Limitations
Rule-based extraction can misread complex layouts; scanned documents need OCR; the taxonomy is finite and cyber-security focused;
semantic models can carry bias; experimental weights depend on the evaluation dataset; injection detection is pattern-based;
malware scanning is signature-based; the system was evaluated on a limited dataset and one machine; password-reset e-mail is not implemented.

## Future scope
OCR for scanned resumes; multilingual support; a learned section and entity model trained on labelled data; a learned ranking model
with calibrated scores; larger-scale human-labelled datasets and inter-annotator agreement; Redis-backed rate limiting and a key-management
service; automated retention reports; accessibility audit; expanded fairness analysis with ethics approval.
